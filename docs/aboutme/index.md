#About Me

My name is Brandon Renovales, and I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate student that intends to pursue a Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering Sciences at UNCC. My studies focus on purpose driven analysis and results that align with streamlined industry standards to maximize efficiency and usefulness of mechanical systems. I was originally introduced to engineering by my fascination with any and all systems that contained physical moving parts, from small console controllers to large automobiles and heavy equipment. I enjoy learning about anything and everything for the sake of gaining a better understanding to the essential questions of what, why, who, and how. Why do these products exist? For what purpose? For who are they intended? How are they designed to achieve the desired metrics? These questions drive the very foundation the world is built on, and they are meant to be expanded. 

##What does it mean to defend an engineering decision : and do you currently know how to do it?

To defend an engineering decision is to be able to research, design, and implement a process or system and to be able to stand by that decision supported by notes, numerous study efforts, and data such that any and all design choices can be justified for their dedicated purpose or function. I would honestly say it is not a skill I can confidently implement at the moment due to the overanalyzing that tends to overcomplicate systems or designs when you let it take over. Many engineers cannot stand by their designs due to the fact that any doubt presented carries the risk of instilling lack of confidence in the readiness of the design and will cause many to revise or restart a process. I would most likely fall in the class of revisions not necessarily for doubt of completion, but for doubt that any given decision was the best to take.
