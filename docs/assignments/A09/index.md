# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective
In this assignment, students are tasked with designing a pulley system by hand that will hold a polyurethane belt in tension between two pulleys supported by a frame/bracket. Half the design constraints are provided, while the other half are to be selected by the student to follow in their calculations. ## Analyze
The assignment provides the following constraints: two pulleys that are both 3 inches in diameter, the rotational speed must be between 1000-2000 rpm, a static belt tension of between 4-6lbf, pulley must be aluminum, abs, or steel, and the design overall must have a minimum safety factor of 3. Based on the selected and given constraints, static and dynamic analysis of the pulley in tension must be calculated to determine a minimum shaft diameter that will then lead into selecting an appropriate journal bearing that will suit the rotational assembly for support and lubrication.

## Decide
For this assignment, I chose a static belt tension of 5 lbf, with the pulleys having a center distance of 5 inches, a rotational speed of 1000 rpm with aluminum as the pulley material. I started with determining the circumference and diameter needed for my pulley belt, based on its static tension and distance of the pulley centers. I then used the force from before to calculate torque and use that as minimum shaft diameter while taking into account the safety factor for all the forces in the design. I then selected an appropriate journal bearing for the application made out of rubber. The final step was to design a frame for the pulleys. ## Communicate
In this assignment, I learned how the interaction between belt and pulley can produce a calculated torque value that changes based on static tension and is affected by diameter of pulleys and their distance. I also learned how to properly size a belt to a given system such that it would withstand the forces put in play for a desired result. I learned how to further develop systems pursuant one another to create a final result in a design. This assignment took about 3-4 hours.
