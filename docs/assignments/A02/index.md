# A2 – Truss Stress Analysis

## Objective

The objectives of this assignment include designing a truss made of a500 steel or similar material to withstand the stresses implemented by 2 forces at points C and D. We are also tasked with analyzing our designed truss with free-body diagrams and static equilibrium to determine the magnitude of the forces on both the beams and the pins that make up the structure to then calculate the required cross-sectional area with a predetermined safety factor. The estimated weight of the truss is also to be calculated and compared with a CAD model matching the original design.
