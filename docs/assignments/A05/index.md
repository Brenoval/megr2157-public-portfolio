## Analyze

## Decide
For this design experiment, I chose to use ASTM A36 Steel which has a Young's Modulus of 36ksi and a Shear Modulus of 11.5ksi. I also chose to make the bracket out of ASTM A36 steel with an arbitrary length at parts A and C/D/E of 2 inches. I then parametrically designed feature by feature using FBD's and symbolic solutions followed by numeric solutions to get dimensions for the entire part. I then ran a separate set of analysis for deformation to then compare the results. My written work will be provided below, followed by a CAD model of the bracket designed with stress analysis.
