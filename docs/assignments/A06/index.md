# A6 – [Bracket Drawing Part 1]

## Objective
For this week's assignment, students are tasked with creating a full model and multiview sketch of the bracket designed during A5 with its respective dimensions relative to their stress and strain analysis. The drawing is to then be dimensioned appropriately based on ANSI engineering standards used in class.

## Decide
For this assignment, I have bypassed the analyze section and chosen to mainstream the process by using the decide section straight away. Due to my issues with the deflection analysis, I chose to model my bracket using the stress analysis performed in the previous week. I started with a new, more streamlined solid model of the bracket with all the same dimensions listed in last week's stress analysis. I then proceeded to open a new part drawing to project my model as an ANSI standard drawing showcasing the top, front, and right side views as my significant faces to be dimensioned. Dimensions for each feature were carefully lay out as to not overlap in unreadable fashion and to not have any repeating dimensions to increase readability.
