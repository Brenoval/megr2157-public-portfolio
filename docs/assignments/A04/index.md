## Objective

The objective of this assignment is to determine the functional properties of a motor mount design taking into account the forces the mount will be withstanding as well as a safety factor to prevent the assembly from collapsing under load. The mount will first be designed by hand and then entered into a CAD model to parametrically check for stresses and deflections.

## Analyze

First, we must analyze the motor the mount is to hold up. It is a round stepper motor with four bolt holes around the outer diameter. The shaft is 6mm in diameter, the inner diameter of the motor is 18mm, and the outer edge where the bolt holes lie is 22mm in diameter. There is a load "P" applied to the shaft that is equal to 300 N. The safety factor determined is 3 and accounts for the holes in the design of the mount. We have 3 materials to choose from: ABS, PETG, and PLA. 

## Decide

I chose to design my mount out of ABS, and selected measurements for area based on the dimensions of the motor, which will be pictured below.
